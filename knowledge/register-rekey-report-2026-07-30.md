## Findings

**Summerfield 2025 is claimed by the register but present in no draft state.** The row `summerfield-2025` carries `2.4 (draft 2.4)`, yet the work appears neither in the current draft nor in the manuscript as it was intaken at `e30bedb`, which held 68 References entries before the pruning. The sanitation report's list of 28 pruned entries does not name it either, so the loss happened before the sanitation pass and is unrecorded. The work is the source of the confabulation term and `knowledge/paper.md` still cites it, so only the draft parenthesis is affected. Whether the operator dropped the sentence deliberately in Fassung 4 is the open question behind the row.

**Two `draft: References only` rows became citations without a recorded decision.** `flanders-2019` and `pichler-2022` were marked References-only on 2026-07-29 with the note that the draft cites the 2015 companion chapter instead. The Fassung 4 intake already carries `(Pichler and Reiter 2022)` in §1 and `(Flanders and Jannidis 2019)` in §1.1, and `flanders-2015` was pruned as uncited. The register's note for `flanders-2019` therefore describes a state the manuscript has left. Both entries came in with the operator's revision, so the register is simply older than the intake.

**Four works dropped out of the body and survive only in footnotes.** `galey-2010` and `whitelaw-2015` now stand in fn26 alone, `wilkinson-2016` in fn7, `borek-2021` in fn1. For Whitelaw the register recorded two body sections, 3.1 and 4.2, and both are gone; the work carries no in-text citation in the current draft. This affects what a claim grounded in those works can be said to support in the manuscript.

**The Pollin lettering diverges from the register's suffix table in one place.** The suffix table gives the draft's letters for five self-citations, and all five hold in the current draft. The table's row for the ZfdG survey says "letterless, Pollin et al. 2025", which the draft carries at §1. The remaining discrepancy is one of location rather than lettering; the register puts three of the five in the genealogy footnote `[^8]`, and the current draft's fn8 is the Bookkeeping Ontology note with no Pollin citation. All three now stand in the body of §2.

**The sanitation report's pruning count reconciles exactly.** Twenty-five of the 28 pruned works hold a register row, and the three that do not are Marwick et al. 2018, whose inventory row sits in `knowledge/state`, Pollin 2023, which has no row anywhere, and Pollin 2026c, which the sanitation pass merged into the Pollin 2024 workshop deposit.
