# Handover point

The handover point is where a project takes on obligations the prototype does not carry, and from there the standards of Research Software Engineering apply to the artefact with their demands on testing, sustainability, and maintenance. Section 4.3 carries the definition.

It is no measure of size. Five axes tip independently of one another, the duty of maintenance beyond a project's own run time, use by third parties with an expectation of support, multi-user operation over shared state, responsibility for data belonging to others, and institutional deployment with a commitment to availability. The standards apply according to what the artefact has become, irrespective of what it was built with.

The point was recast on 2026-07-25 from a size boundary into a change of the obligation regime, which removed its conflict with the statement in Section 3.2 that the exit point of the iteration is a spectrum.
