# Distillation

Distillation is the third phase of the method and compresses what Exploration taught into the document set, under the principle of maximum information at minimum tokens. It ends when a new agent instance, given only the documents and the data, could take up the project's logic and work from it without additional explanation. Section 3.2 carries the definition.

Two grounds stand behind the phase and they are of different kinds. Context Rot supplies the technical rationale, the non-uniform degradation of model performance as input length grows (Section 2.3). The theoretical licence comes from the abbreviation property of models, since omitting what is irrelevant to a purpose is constitutive of modelling and precedes any context-window limit (Section 2.4).

The exit condition was weakened on 2026-07-25 from reconstructing the project to taking its logic up and working from it, because reconstruction contradicted the statement on non-determinism in Section 6.3.
