# Documented grounds of acceptance

The documented grounds of acceptance are the record behind the accountable decision that an identifiable state is adequate for a stated purpose under documented criteria and limitations. They identify the intended purpose, the applicable criteria, the accepting person or group, the known limitations, a version reference, and the referenced research-data state where one applies. Validation, assessment, agentic review and Critical Expert verification contribute evidence to that decision, and none of them constitutes it.

The grounds are one of the four things a promptotype connects. Narrower names are used where a specific record is described, an acceptance note, a dated journal entry, a numbered decision record, a Verification Document, or an equivalent project record. Casual alternation among basis of acceptance, verification record, recorded checks, acceptance record and acceptance evidence is avoided.

The term belongs to the method's own vocabulary and carries no external source.
