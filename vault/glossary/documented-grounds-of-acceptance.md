# Documented grounds of acceptance

The documented grounds of acceptance are the record behind the accountable decision that an identifiable state is adequate for a stated purpose under documented criteria and limitations. They identify the intended purpose, the applicable criteria, the accepting person or group, the known limitations, a version reference, and the referenced research-data state where one applies. Validation, assessment, agentic review and Critical Expert verification contribute evidence to that decision, and none of them constitutes it.

The phrase is the canonical wording set in `knowledge/paper-knowledge.md` (§4.15) for the Abstract, the definition, the method, the figures and the Conclusion; §6.6 gives the content and §4.14 makes the grounds one of the four things a promptotype connects. Narrower names are used where a specific record is described, an acceptance note, a dated journal entry, a numbered decision record, a Verification Document, or an equivalent project record. The definition document rules out casual alternation among basis of acceptance, verification record, recorded checks, acceptance record and acceptance evidence.

The canonical `knowledge/paper.md` does not carry the phrase. Its Section 3.2 ends the iteration on the acceptance criteria of the requirements document together with the verification checks of Section 6.2, and Section 6.1 places the published state under the obligations of Section 4.1, among them the provenance declaration and the versioned documents. The term carries no external source.
