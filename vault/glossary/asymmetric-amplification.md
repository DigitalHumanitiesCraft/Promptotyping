# Asymmetric amplification

Asymmetric amplification names the dynamic in which LLMs amplify computer-based research work along existing gradients of expertise, access, and epistemic selectivity, so that the same technology produces autonomy where verification competence exists and dependence where it is absent. Section 6.4 carries the term as the ethical dimension of the access limit, and the author set it in [[10_distillates/publications/pollin-2026a-asymmetric-amplification#^s2]].

The three gradients are the paper's systematisation. The source states the asymmetry together with its facets of access, competence, and resources and names no gradients.
