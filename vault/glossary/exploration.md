# Exploration

Exploration is the second phase of the method and probes the interface between data and research context, asking whether the research question can be mapped onto the available data structure. The researcher inspects the data directly, scripts written for the purpose traverse the corpus and render compact aggregations, and the model generates mapping hypotheses. The phase ends with a documented understanding of what is feasible, what is not, and why, and it is skipped where the data is familiar to the researcher and Preparation settled these questions. Section 3.2 carries the definition.

The phase was widened on 2026-07-25 by the researcher's own inspection of the data and by the script-based aggregation, and it received the skip condition in the same pass. The formulation that dead ends are positive findings was struck.
