# Promptotype

A promptotype is the identifiable state produced by an accepted Promptotyping iteration. It is a relation among four things, the maintained project knowledge, the digital research artefact derived from it, the referenced research-data state, and the documented grounds of acceptance. None of these alone is a promptotype, so a functioning interface, a generated codebase, a prompt sequence, a notebook, a repository or a Custom GPT falls short of the term while the relation is missing.

Acceptance closes an iteration for a defined purpose and implies none of the following:

- final truth;
- completion of the research project;
- publication;
- long-term maintainability;
- institutional readiness;
- suitability for purposes outside the acceptance decision.

An earlier reading named the promptotype the product of one iteration, regenerated from the documents rather than maintained, whose yield is the understanding it produced about the data, the research context and the range of what can be made from them. That reading names an artefact where the definition above names a relation. The asymmetry it describes survives in the current reading, since the project knowledge is what is maintained and the artefact is derived from it.

The earlier reading dates from 2026-07-25, and the relational definition supersedes it.
