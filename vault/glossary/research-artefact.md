# Research artefact

A research artefact is what the documents are turned into for a project's research context, bound to the data of that project and working on it. It is most often software through which the data becomes explorable, analysable, or editable, and it equally takes the form of a workflow, a data model, a derived dataset, or a description generated from the data. Sections 1 and 4.1 carry the definition.

Section 4.1 delimits the artefact against the generic tool. A tool serves many projects at the level of generality all of them share, while the artefact's scholarly standing comes from its binding to one project and to the distinctions that project's data model carries.

The genus was opened on 2026-07-25, with software as the regular case. The rejected alternative bound the artefact to a research question; it was dropped because the exploratory case brings the question forth only in the working, so the binding runs through the research context. The wider genus also removed the conflict with the deterministically generated document, which the genus now covers.
