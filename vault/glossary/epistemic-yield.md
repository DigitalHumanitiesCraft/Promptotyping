# Epistemic yield

The epistemic yield of a case is what it produced about the object of study or about the means of inquiry. Sections 5.1 and 5.3 carry it as a fixed column of the case schema, and the worked cases separate the two kinds, since one produced a philological result while the others produced research artefacts and left the material where it was.

The term rests on no external source and was not discussed in the terminology pass of 2026-07-25.
