# Promptotyping

Promptotyping is an iterative, document-driven context engineering method situated within agentic engineering, through which individual researchers and small teams translate their structured research data into research artefacts. Its unit of work is a small set of versioned Markdown documents from which an LLM-based agent derives the artefact. Section 1 carries the canonical definition and Section 3.2 the process. The name and the first public description are the author's [[10_distillates/publications/pollin-2025d-promptotyping-blog#^s1]], and the four-phase form is restated in [[10_distillates/publications/pollin-2026b-lisa-positioning#^s1]].

Against Vibe Coding the method holds the structured preparation and exploration that precede generation, the persistent documents, and the verification at defined checkpoints (Section 2.5). Against Spec-Driven Development the difference lies in what the data document describes, sources that precede the artefact and carry their own semantics and their own uncertainty, and in the kind of authority the reading scholar holds, which is authority over the subject matter.

Section 3.1 fixes the status of the definition. The method was distilled from practice and the paper consolidates it after the fact, so the earlier descriptions are precursors and this paper is the canonical fixing.
