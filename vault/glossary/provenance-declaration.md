# Provenance declaration

A provenance declaration states that an artefact was generated, from which documents, with which models and tools, and how it was verified. Section 4.1 sets it as a requirement on every artefact of the type, and the analogy it invokes is the edition disclosing its editorial principles.

The requirement rests on no external source. It is where the reproducibility position of Section 6.3 becomes a property of the artefact itself, since binary artefact identity lies outside reproducibility and the decision trail is what the declaration exposes.
