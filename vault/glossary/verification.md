# Verification

Verification is what the domain expert decides against the sources and against scholarly judgement, and it does not delegate. Section 6.2 carries the definition, and its footnote there discloses the departure from the established use.

The counterpart is [[glossary/validation]]. The pair operates at three levels, data fidelity, requirement satisfaction, and design conformance, and each level carries its own zone of agent autonomy. Design conformance stays in the expert zone, together with interpretation, contextualisation, and the judgement of whether the right questions are being asked.

The fixing was decided by the operator on 2026-07-25 with disclosure. It departs from ISO 9000 and IEEE 1012, where verification is the check against the specification and validation the check against the intended use, and it stands against formal methods and current LLM post-training, which reserve verification for the deterministic case. The ground is the word root *verus*, a claim to truth that a judgement must carry. A targeted search found no published work with the same cut. The reach of the fixing is the checking of generated output, and the ordinary sense in which cases validate a method stays untouched.
