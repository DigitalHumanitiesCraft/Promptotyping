# Research context

The research context is the questions a project asks of its material, the domain knowledge behind them, and the standards its data follows. Section 2.5 carries the definition.

The term ran through the paper in use before it was defined and was resolved on 2026-07-25. It now carries the binding of the research artefact and the yield of the promptotype, where the narrower research question stood before.
