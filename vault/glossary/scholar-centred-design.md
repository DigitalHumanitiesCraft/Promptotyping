# Scholar-Centred Design

Scholar-Centred Design is the systematic, iterative methodology that integrates the practices, needs, and expertise of academic scholars into the development of scholarly tools and frameworks, combining user-centred design with requirements engineering. Sections 2.6 and 3.3 carry it as the methodological core of the author's dissertation, where it is defined [[20_distillates/publications/pollin-2025b-dissertation#^s1]].

Its instruments are personas, epics, and user stories from deep-dive sessions with domain experts. The paper reads the requirements document of the method as the continuation of this practice by other means, so the term enters the paper as genealogy.
