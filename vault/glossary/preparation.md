# Preparation

Preparation is the first phase of the method and collects and structures all materials before technical decisions are made, among them research data in original formats, documentation of standards and data models, research questions, and domain knowledge. It typically begins with requirements engineering, and it ends when the repository structure exists, the source data is accessible, and initial requirements are documented as a structured starting point. Section 3.2 carries the definition.
