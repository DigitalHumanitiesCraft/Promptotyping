# Tool positivism

Tool positivism is the assumption that research problems reduce to tooling problems. Section 2.2 introduces it as the paper's own counter-term, added to digital tool criticism as a second corrective and applied to the method itself, and it rests on no external source.

The rejection has a positive form. The tool is a derived artefact, and the epistemic centre is the modelled research data together with the documents that describe, specify, and justify what is to be built from it.
