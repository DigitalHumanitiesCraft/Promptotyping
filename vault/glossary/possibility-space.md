# Possibility space

The possibility space is the set of options that could be generated and that the Critical Expert has the task of examining, including the questions not asked and the alternatives not generated. Sections 2.5, 3.2, and 5.3 carry the usage, and the term rests on no external source.

The convention problem of Section 6.4 is what makes the space hard to reach. Generated output falls back on learned conventions, so during Implementation the researcher requests radically different designs that run against them and surface options the defaults suppress.
