# Knowledge Document

A knowledge document is a structured unit of knowledge distilled from raw material and optimised as a context artefact for work shared between people and language models, in the vocabulary of context engineering a context compression made durable. Three properties define it, dual readability, compactness, and portability. Section 3.3 carries the definition, which is a working definition of the author from his teaching on context engineering and rests on no external source.

The term is the genus and the Promptotyping Document is its project-bound species. Until 2026-07-25 the declarative subtype of the document typology carried the same name; it was renamed Declarative Document in that pass so that the genus name stays unambiguous, at the cost of follow-up work in the template catalogue, in the repository, and in the anchors.
