# Verification Document

A Verification Document records what claim was checked, against what evidence, by what procedure, and with what verdict. Sections 3.3, 4.1, and 5.1 carry the function, which the paper promoted from an observed habit across several repositories into a document function of its own; the quantitative claims of Section 5 are covered by one, and the FAIR4RS audit of Section 4.1 is deposited as one.

The document is declarative in the typology, which is where knowledge about the subject matter belongs. Its delimitation against the Verification Interface of Section 4.2 and against the three checking levels of Section 6.2 is still open.
