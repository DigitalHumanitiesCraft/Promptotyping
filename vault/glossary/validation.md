# Validation

Validation is what a formal rule decides, a schema, a test, or a constraint, and it runs unsupervised because the feedback loop is closed. Section 6.2 carries the definition.

The counterpart is [[glossary/verification]]. This half of the pair meets the established use, where schema languages and the validation practice of encoded text decide conformance by rule; the departure from the norm tradition lies on the verification side and is disclosed in the footnote to Section 6.2. The ground is the word root *validus*, what holds under a stated rule. The fixing was decided by the operator on 2026-07-25.
