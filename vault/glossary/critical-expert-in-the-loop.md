# Critical Expert in the Loop

The Critical Expert in the Loop is the role the method presupposes, distinguished from the generic human in the loop by requiring domain expertise together with awareness of LLM-specific failure modes. The task extends beyond checking outputs into the possibility space that was not explored, the questions not asked and the alternatives not generated. Section 2.5 carries the definition, which the author introduced with a double loop of human review and elicited machine self-critique [[20_distillates/publications/pollin-2025c-critical-vibing#^s2]].

The role owns the verification duties of Section 6.2 and is the human side of the division of labour of Section 3.4. Two failure modes are named as structural in current models, sycophancy and confabulation.
