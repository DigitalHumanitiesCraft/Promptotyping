# Promptotyping Paper Vault

Human entry point of this vault. The vault carries the provenance layer beneath the Promptotyping paper (a scholarly synthesis); every load-bearing statement is anchored to its source material, and the checking state of every statement is readable at the statement itself.

## Read the deliverable

- The paper lives canonically in this repository as `knowledge/paper.md`; `_content/paper/` is the derived publication form the site renders, regenerated from the canonical text only after release. The chapter register in [[knowledge/state]] maps the paper's sections to the claims here.

## How to read a status

`grounded` means an agent produced the anchor structure. `validated` means deterministic checks and an adversarial machine review passed. `verified` means the human expert confirmed it; only this is evidence. `contested` means sources conflict, which is itself information.
