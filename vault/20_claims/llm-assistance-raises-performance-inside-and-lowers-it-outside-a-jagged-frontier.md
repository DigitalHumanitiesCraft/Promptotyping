# LLM assistance raises performance on tasks inside a jagged capability frontier and lowers it on tasks outside, within one workflow.

## Statement

In a field experiment with 758 consultants, access to a frontier LLM raised productivity and output quality on the consulting tasks that lay inside the boundary of AI capability and lowered the rate of correct solutions on a task that lay outside it. The two effects belong to one flow of interdependent tasks, and difficulty as a human judges it does not tell the two groups of tasks apart. The study names this uneven boundary the jagged technological frontier.
