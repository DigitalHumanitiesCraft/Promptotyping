# In LLM-assisted work reproducibility shifts from identical repetition to traceable justification.

## Statement

Reproducibility is reframed from identical repetition to a traceable, documented justification, comparable to qualitative research. This reframing meets a prior condition of the humanities, where reproducible research in the strong sense has limited currency because the scholar's subject position is bound to the scholarship. Checked against the deliverable on 2026-07-25, the reframing itself is still in use there, in the reproducibility passage that locates reproducibility in the documents, the process record, and the deterministic pipeline.
