# The Quality User Story framework fixes thirteen criteria for user-story quality, grouped syntactic, semantic and pragmatic, and scoped either to one story or to the set.

## Statement

Lucassen, Dalpiaz, van der Werf and Brinkkemper define thirteen quality criteria for user stories and organise them in Lindland's three categories. Syntactic quality holds well-formed, atomic and minimal; semantic quality holds conceptually sound, problem-oriented, unambiguous and conflict-free; pragmatic quality holds full sentence, estimatable, unique, uniform, independent and complete. A second classification records whether a criterion is judged against a single story or against the set, which puts conflict-free, unique, uniform, independent and complete at the level of the document rather than the line. Every criterion judges the intrinsic quality of the story text and none of them asks whether the story is true of the user it names.
