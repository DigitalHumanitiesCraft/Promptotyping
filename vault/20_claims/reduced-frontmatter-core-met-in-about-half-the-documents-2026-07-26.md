# The reduced six-field frontmatter core is met in about half of the documents that carry frontmatter, as of the snapshot of 2026-07-26.

## Statement

A survey of 2026-07-26 over 490 documents with YAML frontmatter in 40 local knowledge bases found all six fields of the reduced mandatory core present together in 233 of them, roughly half. `method` is the binding field, so joint fulfilment follows the rate of that one field closely. The figures describe the operator's local working copies on that date. The run kept no script and no file list, so it cannot be recomputed on a different stock, and the claim carries the rate as a dated self-observation of the method's own uptake rather than as a checkable external measurement.
