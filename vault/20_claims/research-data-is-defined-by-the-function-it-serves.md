# What makes data research data is the function it serves, not a list of data types.

## Statement

The German-language digital humanities have no uniform definition of research data, and the functional route decides the question instead: data of a research process counts as research data where it is needed to justify the findings, to sustain their claim to truth, or to reproduce the individual steps. This grounds the paper's Section 2.2 sentence that research data is constituted by the function it serves, with grounding, examination of validity claims, and reproduction as the three duties named there. The source withholds a definition on purpose and offers these criteria as selection criteria, so the claim carries the functional cut and not a definition attributed to this source.
