# The generalised DEPCHA dashboard serves broad overviews and can flatten the context-specific detail that social and economic historians require.

## Statement

The dissertation records the limit of the generalised Dashboard View of DEPCHA in its own terms. The view helps with broad overviews and can overlook what social and economic historians require, flattening context-specific detail; against generalist interfaces of this kind it sets scholar-centred, flexible and interactive environments, which the two experimental interfaces exemplify. This grounds the point Section 1 of the paper uses as the origin of the method, that a shared ontology can normalise heterogeneous datasets without a single interface serving the perspectives built on them.
