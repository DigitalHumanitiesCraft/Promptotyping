# Distillate: Frontmatter-core survey 2026-07-26

A dated snapshot of how far the reduced six-field frontmatter core is met across the operator's local knowledge bases; the paper's Section 3.3 takes from it the finding that the reduced core is met in about half of the documents. The source is a self-observation of the author's own working environment and carries the reproducibility limit stated in its own text, so the statements below are grounded in a snapshot rather than in an externally checkable measurement.

## Core statements

- The survey base is 490 documents with YAML frontmatter across 40 knowledge bases. [[00_representation/documents/frontmatter-core-survey-2026-07-26#^base]] ^s1
- All six fields of the reduced mandatory core are present together in 233 of the 490 documents, roughly half of the base. [[00_representation/documents/frontmatter-core-survey-2026-07-26#^core]] ^s2
- `method` is the binding field of the six, the one whose absence accounts for most of the shortfall in joint fulfilment. [[00_representation/documents/frontmatter-core-survey-2026-07-26#^method]] ^s3
- The field `zweck` appears in no document of the base. [[00_representation/documents/frontmatter-core-survey-2026-07-26#^zweck]] ^s4
- The field `template`, which addresses the authoritative template specification, appears in 79 documents. [[00_representation/documents/frontmatter-core-survey-2026-07-26#^template]] ^s5
- The field `generated-with` appears in 102 documents with fourteen distinct value variants. [[00_representation/documents/frontmatter-core-survey-2026-07-26#^generatedwith]] ^s6
- Deterministic generation by a script is marked in two documents of the base. [[00_representation/documents/frontmatter-core-survey-2026-07-26#^deterministic]] ^s7
- The run read local working copies on 2026-07-26, retained no script and recorded no fixed file stock, so its figures are not reproducible without the same working copies in the same state. [[00_representation/documents/frontmatter-core-survey-2026-07-26#^limits]] ^s8

## Terms

- **Reduced mandatory core**: the six frontmatter fields `title`, `project`, `method`, `status`, `created`, `updated` that the convention requires after the reduction recorded in the frontmatter-practice note of 2026-05. [[00_representation/documents/frontmatter-core-survey-2026-07-26#^core]]
- **Binding field**: the field of the core whose absence decides most cases of non-fulfilment, so that the joint rate follows its own rate closely. [[00_representation/documents/frontmatter-core-survey-2026-07-26#^method]]

## Open questions

- The base excludes documents without any frontmatter, which the audit of 2026-07-19 found in roughly a third of the knowledge bases. The rate of about half therefore describes the documents that already carry frontmatter and understates the distance to the convention across the whole stock.
- The survey does not distinguish repositories that follow the convention from repositories that never adopted it, so the rate mixes two populations with different obligations.
- No script and no file list survive the run, so the figures cannot be recomputed. A repetition would measure a changed stock, and the two runs would not be comparable as a time series.
- The source is a self-observation of the method's author about the method's own uptake. Whether the rate holds beyond this working environment is untested.
