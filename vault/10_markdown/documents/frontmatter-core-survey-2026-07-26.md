# 2026-07-26 - Survey of the reduced frontmatter core across the local knowledge bases

Handover record of a survey run on 2026-07-26 by a subagent over the local working copies of the repositories under `C:\Users\Chrisi\Documents\GitHub\`. The survey asked how far the reduced mandatory frontmatter core of the Promptotyping Documents convention, the six fields `title`, `project`, `method`, `status`, `created`, `updated`, is met in the documents that carry YAML frontmatter at all.

## Method and its limits

The run read the local working copies as they stood on 2026-07-26. It did not read the remote repositories, and it counted only documents that carry a YAML frontmatter block, so documents without frontmatter are outside the base rather than counted as failures. No script was retained, and no fixed file stock was recorded, so the figures are not reproducible on a different machine or at a later date without the same working copies in the same state. The survey is a dated snapshot of the author's own working environment, and its evidential force is limited accordingly. ^limits

## Base

490 documents with YAML frontmatter, distributed over 40 knowledge bases. ^base

## Findings

All six fields of the reduced core are present together in 233 of the 490 documents, roughly half of the base. ^core

`method` is the binding field. It is the one of the six whose absence accounts for most of the shortfall, so joint fulfilment of the core stands or falls with it. ^method

The field `zweck` appears in no document of the base. ^zweck

The field `template`, which addresses the authoritative template specification, appears in 79 documents. ^template

The field `generated-with` appears in 102 documents, carrying fourteen distinct value variants, so the field is in use without a controlled vocabulary behind it. ^generatedwith

Deterministic generation by a script is marked in two documents of the base. ^deterministic
