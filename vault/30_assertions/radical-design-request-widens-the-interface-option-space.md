# A deliberately outlandish design request widens the space of interface options the session considers.

## Statement

At the close of the recorded session the author orders a design in the style of Frank Herbert's Dune, classes the step as unprofessional, and states that trying radically different things with such interfaces can yield interface elements and designs nobody had thought of, which widens the possibility space and lets the first iteration teach something for the next phase. A single prompt produces the science-fiction interface, whose sense he leaves open, and he names the exploration of the possibility space as what Promptotyping is about. The claim carries the widening as the source states it. That the tool's defaults reproduce design conventions is not stated in this source; the nearest thing it carries is the unrequested dark mode as a typical addition. The witness is the method's author on his own session.
