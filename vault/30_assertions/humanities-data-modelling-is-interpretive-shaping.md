# Humanities data models represent the interpretive shaping choices scholars make about their material.

## Statement

Data models in the digital humanities represent the shaping choices scholars make when they represent and analyse their material, and modelling makes explicit the assumptions a scholar holds about the nature of a text or artefact. This grounds the paper's point that the contributors to Flanders and Jannidis treat humanities data as the product of interpretive modelling rather than as given.
