# The recorded practice carries two expert judgements in the loop, one over the subject matter and one over the development side.

## Statement

At the point where the tool analyses the screenshots of the running interface, the author places the Critical Expert in the Loop and gives that role the question whether what is shown is actually right; the analysis the tool returns is hallucinated content that could well be correct, and the problem stays. Where a remaining defect at a range element has to be addressed, the author names an Expert Developer in the Loop, because addressing the element by its identifier and passing it as a hash presupposes HTML-specific knowledge. The two are told apart by what each has to know, domain competence for the judgement over the display and web-technical competence for the judgement over the fix. The source is the method's author speaking about a session he conducted alone, so the separation is one he draws, and the recording shows both judgements held by the same person.
