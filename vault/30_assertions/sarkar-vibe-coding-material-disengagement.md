# The first empirical study of vibe coding describes it as an iterative goal-satisfaction cycle under selective human oversight.

## Statement

The first empirical study of vibe coding, based on over eight hours of think-aloud video, characterises the practice as an iterative goal-satisfaction cycle of prompting, rapid evaluation and manual editing, and as an early form of material disengagement in which practitioners orchestrate AI-produced code while keeping selective, strategic oversight. This grounds the paper's treatment of vibe coding as an exploratory mode retained inside the method rather than rejected.
