# The research software support institutions provide falls short of what researchers need.

## Statement

Across a survey of 1,149 researchers, primarily in the United States, the research-software-engineering, financial, and infrastructure support that institutions provide is inadequate to the respondents' needs, funding for software development activities is overwhelmingly reported as insufficient, and fewer than a third rate any surveyed dimension of support as sufficient. This grounds the paper's introductory premise that the institutionalised intermediary answer does not meet demand where it exists. The paper's further step, that the shortfall is larger still for individual researchers and small projects outside institutional attachment, is marked in the text as an inference and is not carried by this source.
