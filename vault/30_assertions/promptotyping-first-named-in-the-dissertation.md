# The term Promptotyping was introduced in the dissertation, which devotes Section 7.4 to the method.

## Statement

The dissertation introduces the concept of Promptotyping in answer to its fourth research question on LLM-supported interface development and carries a section of its own for it, 7.4 LLM-Supported Prototyping and Exploration: Promptotyping. It also names the Promptotype Documents as context-compressed Markdown files capturing the relevant semantics. The two public contributions of 2025 and 2026 develop the term after that point rather than coining it, which corrects the earlier reading that the blog post was its first use.
