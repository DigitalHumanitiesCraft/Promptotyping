# TaDiRAH was revised into a formalised, machine-readable SKOS knowledge organization system, released as version 2.0.

## Statement

A revision of TaDiRAH by a different author team transformed the taxonomy into a machine-readable, formalised vocabulary, realised in SKOS on pragmatic grounds and extensible through Wikidata, and released as version 2.0 as a knowledge organization system that improves the FAIR properties of digital humanities research data and assets. This grounds the paper's reference to the current, addressable vocabulary at vocabs.dariah.eu, as distinct from the original classification that [[30_assertions/tadirah-classifies-dh-research-activities]] carries.
