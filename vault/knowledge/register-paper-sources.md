# Register of Paper Sources

Working register of the bibliography-traceability sweep (operator decision 2026-07-23, see [[knowledge/specification]]). One row per work that is or was cited by the paper (`knowledge/paper.md`, the five-chapter text promoted on 2026-07-30). Canonical inventory rows stay in [[knowledge/state]]; this register is the sweep's overview and backlog. Coordinator-maintained; production subagents report row updates and never write this file.

Access classes. **A** openly readable online (agent-ingestible). **B** operator-supplied local copy required (place in `00_sources/`). **C** not accessible (paywall or print without local copy); no distillate is faked, the paper's citation stands on its own and the row keeps the gap visible.

Status values. `registered` (row exists) → `csl` (reference record in `references/`) → `ingested` (source in `00_sources/`) → `distilled` (distillate with quote-checked statements) → `claimed` (≥1 claim grounded and MOC-listed).

Paper roles name the sections and footnotes of `knowledge/paper.md`, the five-chapter text promoted on 2026-07-30, in which the work is cited (`2.3`, `fn24`). A work the paper no longer cites keeps its row with the role `orphaned`, so that its distillate and claims stay findable as checked material without a current use in the paper.

Promotion re-keying of 2026-07-30. With the promotion of the sanitised five-chapter manuscript, the role column was re-keyed to it alone and the two-track parenthesis of 2026-07-29 was dropped, as the column protocol of that day prescribed. Row notes dated before the promotion describe the two-track state in their own present tense; they are adoption and provenance records and were not rewritten. The same pass moved the sixteen rows registered on 2026-07-30 and `stachowiak-1973` to status `csl` (CSL intake with landing-page re-verification, run 2026-07-30), confirmed six proposed access classes as A on publisher-deposited licence metadata or an open author version (`szymanski-2025`, `green-2022`, `fakhoury-2024`, `benito-santos-2026`, `hertzum-2024`, and `schoen-1996` on the confirmed Stanford full text), and entered `glazer-2024` and `hubert-2025` as the two carriers of footnote 24. After the apparatus cleaning the paper's References carry no uncited entry.

Three works the paper cites keep their inventory row in [[knowledge/state]] rather than here, `lucassen-2016` (2.2), `saltzer-1975` (2.3) and `marwick-2018` (orphaned since the promotion, pruned as uncited). They entered with the site-vocabulary sweep of 2026-07-26 and are distilled there. They are deliberately not duplicated into this register, because the state of a source is recorded in one place only.
