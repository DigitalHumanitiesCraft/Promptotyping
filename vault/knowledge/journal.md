# Journal

Chronological decision history of the vault, append-only, newest entry last. Content documents carry only current state; the reasoning that led there lives here. An entry records a decision, a rejected alternative with the reason, or a calibration result of a check mechanism.

## Entry format

```markdown
## <date> — <one-line subject>

<What was decided or found, why, and what it replaces. Link the affected
documents. Two to ten sentences.>
```

## 2026-07-26 — Site-vocabulary literature sweep, nine sources for five coinages

The public site's glossary carries five terms marked as its own coinages, three of them with the note that the paper does not carry them. The question put to this sweep was whether each attaches to an established name in the research literature and whether a source carries what the entry asserts. Nine publications entered as sources with quote-checked distillates, and ten claims were built under Method, Concepts and ArtefactVerification.

Four of the five terms found a name. Role simulation is role play, in the sense Shanahan, McDonell and Reynolds fix for Nature, and their correction bounds the site's use, because a prompt-set role is a distribution the exchange keeps reshaping rather than an enforced boundary; the graded-permissions half of the same entry is least privilege after Saltzer and Schroeder, and the role split across instances is the assembly-line role assignment of the MetaGPT line. The knowledge-action composition rides on the action-against-cognition axis that Diátaxis states explicitly, with the knowing-how and knowing-that gloss; Diátaxis is citable and open, while the classic loci of the same distinction, Winograd 1975 and Ryle 1949, produced no open copy, so Winograd holds a CSL record without a distillate. Standalone research data is the research compendium of Marwick, Boettiger and Mullen, whose three principles include exactly the read-only separation of data from code that the pattern practises. Claim verification as a document function is a composite; the argumentation vocabulary comes from Toulmin, carried here at second hand through Hitchcock because no open copy of the 1958 book was found, and the record form is closest to the nanopublication of Groth, Gibson and Velterop, while the verdict and the procedure belong to neither and stay the site's own.

The script-versus-LLM division found no single name and a strong pair of anchors from either side. Lucassen et al. 2016 draw the line explicitly for their own tool, admitting the syntactic criteria and those pragmatic ones a rule can decide and excluding the semantic criteria because they require deep understanding of the content; Zheng et al. 2023 mark the other end, where a strong model matches human preference at the rate humans match each other, under four named biases. The division is therefore not a coinage without prior art but a re-statement of a boundary requirements engineering and evaluation research already draw.

One correction to the site falls out of the sweep and is reported rather than made, since this lane writes no site text. The glossary entry `scholar-user-stories` and the practice page both assert eight review criteria for `user-stories.md`, four machine-checkable and three human-only, attributed to the QUS framework. The framework carries thirteen criteria in three categories, its tool implements five analyzers in the reported version, and its arithmetic does not produce the site's numbers under any reading. The criteria list the site needs does not exist in the repository, and this sweep supplies the sourced basis for writing one.

Carrier flags for the verification role. Saltzer and Schroeder is quoted from a third-party HTML transcription, not the IEEE version of record. Groth et al. is quoted from the pre-publication manuscript on the W3C wiki rather than the printed pages 51 to 56. Marwick et al. is quoted from a third-party host of the publisher PDF whose running heads carry no page numbers.
