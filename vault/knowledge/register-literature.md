# Register of Literature Sources

Inventory register of the published works that entered this vault as distillates. One row per work, with its CSL record in `references/`, its access class, its processing status, its distillate and the assertions grounded in it. Sources that are not publications, and the few publications that entered with the site-vocabulary sweep, keep their row in [[knowledge/state]].

Access classes. A is openly readable online. B needs an operator-supplied local copy in `00_sources/`. C is not accessible, so no distillate is made and the gap stays visible.

Status values. `csl` (reference record in `references/`) → `ingested` (source in `00_sources/`) → `distilled` (distillate with quote-checked statements) → `claimed` (at least one assertion grounded and MOC-listed).
