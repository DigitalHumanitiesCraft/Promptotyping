# Figure Prompts

This directory contains the process and acceptance records for the figures of the Promptotyping paper, one file per figure.

The figures were produced with ChatGPT Images 2.0. Generation was iterative: early outputs were inspected for conceptual accuracy, visual hierarchy, terminology, and consistency with the manuscript. Revisions were requested when a figure conflated implementation and acceptance, treated write-back as a linear phase, obscured authority boundaries, or reproduced a poster-like rather than journal-figure layout. Every delivered version, adopted or superseded, is archived under `../candidates/` with an assessment register in `../PROVENANCE.md`.

The prompt texts themselves were not retained; generation ran interactively in ChatGPT and the specifications were not preserved as files. The records here therefore do not reconstruct a prompt history. What they preserve is the intended explanatory purpose, the delivered versions and what the adopted one improved, the accepted output file, and the grounds on which the figure was selected for the manuscript. They are part of the project knowledge base and process record; they do not by themselves reproduce identical images, because model outputs are non-deterministic and the service may change over time.
