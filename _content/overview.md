# Promptotyping

Specification of an iterative, knowledge-driven method organised around four recurrent forms of work for developing project-specific research artefacts from structured research data and maintained project knowledge.

## Start with your goal

- **Understand the method.** Read [Application](#application) for the operational account, then use the [paper](#abschnitt-1-introduction) for the scholarly rationale and evaluation.
- **Evaluate its grounds.** Examine the documented [use cases](#use-cases) and trace the paper's load-bearing assertions through the [Vault](#vault).
- **Apply it to a project.** Follow the [tutorial](#tutorial), use the [canonical Promptotyping skill](#skills-promptotyping), and select the [templates](#vorlagen) whose triggers match your project.

Promptotyping derives implementation from inspectable project knowledge that persists across sessions and receives consequential findings through write-back. Preparation and Exploration examine the material before durable requirements are distilled; small increments then receive deterministic checks and domain judgement before responsible contributors record purpose-specific acceptance. A versioned `knowledge/` folder is the canonical repository convention used by this portal and its templates. The [paper](#paper) provides the scholarly rationale, evidence and limitations.
