# Austrian University Dashboard

## Context

An interactive dashboard over a corpus of Excel files holding Austrian university statistics, built end to end on video as the didactic baseline of the method. The recording is part 2 of the introduction to Promptotyping and is the whole record of the case, which has no public repository. The session runs the complete cycle, exploration of the spreadsheet corpus, a data document, knowledge documents for architecture, user interface and user stories, an overengineering check before implementation, a cut back to a minimum viable product, implementation against milestones, and iteration on the interface through screenshot feedback. A redesign in the visual idiom of Dune sits inside the same session.

## Contribution

The case exists so that the phases can be watched while they happen. Everything the paper states as method appears in the recording in the order it actually occurs, including the judgements that a written description conveys least well.

Two of those judgements are the didactic core. The overengineering check, run as a matrix of what the artefact would have to carry, and the reduction to a minimum viable product that follows it, are both moments in which the session removes scope from a plan the model was willing to build. Watching scope being cut is what distinguishes this material from a walkthrough of a finished result.

The redesign carries a third point. Choosing a visual idiom deliberately, against alternatives, shows the possibility space as something a session explores, where the default outcome is whatever the model produces first.

## Limits

There is no public repository. The documents and the code exist only as they pass across the screen, so nothing about the artefact can be checked independently of the recording. The paper's inventory rule in Section 5.2 admits only projects that left a repository, and this case falls outside the evidence layer by that rule.

The demonstrator is the method's author. The case establishes that the process is demonstrable and settles nothing about whether a scholar who did not develop the method can carry it through, which is the question Section 5.4 opens and Section 6.5 leaves for empirical work.

The material is a spreadsheet corpus. The part of the method that carries a formal data model into an interface stays outside what the recording shows, and a viewer who works with encoded editions or ontologies sees the phases without the modelling problem they were built for.

The session was recorded with one agentic tool at one point in that tool's development. Behaviour that follows from the tool's state at the time cannot be separated in the recording from behaviour that follows from the method, so a viewer needs the recording date to weigh what is shown.
