# Knowledge Organisation across Three Tools

## Context

A recorded workflow that takes a video on the fundamentals of generative AI and carries it through to teaching slides. The content is extracted, verified and synthesised into a knowledge document; an agent then analyses the vault, finds where the document belongs and links it to what is already there; an image model produces an infographic which is improved iteratively against feedback; the result goes into slides.

## Contribution

The case is about tool differentiation. A chat model handles extraction and synthesis, an agentic environment handles the integration into the vault, and an image model handles the graphic and the large-context reading. None of the three substitutes for another, and the workflow only holds because a human sequences them and decides when a step is finished.

The verification standard is the second point. The instruction to list and justify is what turns a synthesis into something checkable, and the session applies it at each handover.

## Limits

The case is a demonstration, and it establishes nothing about the fundamentals it organises. Its subject is the workflow.

Three tools mean three providers and three failure modes, and the session does not test what happens when one of them is unavailable or changes behaviour. The material is a recording, so the intermediate states cannot be inspected.
