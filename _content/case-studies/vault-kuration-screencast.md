# Obsidian Vault Curation

## Context

A recorded curation of a vault that had grown organically and unevenly. The session analyses it programmatically with Python for statistics, orphaned notes and hub documents, merges two documents that covered the same ground, strips quantities and formulas, and updates the links across the whole vault. A second model with a large context window is then tested on the merged state to see whether new research perspectives can be synthesised from it. The rules for future curation are extracted from the work itself.

## Contribution

The session separates what a script can decide from what needs interpretation. Counting notes, finding orphans and rewriting links are algorithmic tasks and belong in Python. Deciding whether two documents cover the same ground, and which formulation survives a merge, needs reading. Putting the boundary in the right place is what makes the curation tractable.

The second contribution concerns links. In a vault worked on by agents, a link is a navigable context path, so link maintenance is context engineering and the curation is an infrastructural task.

## Limits

The case is a demonstration. The synthesis experiment with the large-context model is exploratory, and the session does not establish that the perspectives it produced hold.

The material is a recording, so the vault state before and after cannot be inspected independently. The extracted rules are the transferable part, and they carry only as far as another vault resembles this one.
