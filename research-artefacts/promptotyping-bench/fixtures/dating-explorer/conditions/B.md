# Working protocol B

Apply this compact Promptotyping workflow to the current task:

1. Inspect the material and state what it supports and what remains uncertain.
2. Maintain a short `knowledge/data-specification.md` with the relevant data rules,
   requirements, decisions and their source. Keep fuller source files available.
3. Implement a bounded step and check its result against those requirements.
4. Write consequential corrections and findings back into that knowledge document.
5. Maintain `knowledge/handoff.md` with the current state, checks, unresolved points
   and next work. Read both documents when continuing in a fresh session and revise
   them if the supplied sources or accepted requirements have changed.

Aim for at most 500 words across the two documents; this is a proportionality target,
not an outcome-score rule. Keep your final message brief and identify unresolved
work. The current task and supplied editorial policy govern substantive requirements.
Technical checks and agent self-assessment do not supply human scholarly acceptance.
